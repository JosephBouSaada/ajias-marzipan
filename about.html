One maker, one ingredient

Ajia makes marzipan and nothing else. Not cake with marzipan on it, not a bakery counter with marzipan in the corner: the whole business is one confection, worked by hand into whatever the occasion needs.

How a piece is made

Paste is portioned into rounds, then shaped by hand: petals turn one by one, grapes build bead by bead, and baby bonnets are scored to look knitted.

Carved moulds help with some forms. Colour, gold and finishing are still added by hand.

A tray of sixty roses is sixty separate roses, so lead time matters.

Almonds and sugar. That is the whole list.

Ajia's marzipan has no flour, butter, cream or egg. It is naturally vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free. The chocolate-dipped jars are the exception.

In her own words

I began making marzipan when I could not find ready-made pieces that felt special enough. My first creation was a gift for a family engagement party. Friends and family soon began asking for pieces of their own.

Today, I shape every order by hand and tailor the colours, flavours and details to each celebration. To me, marzipan is more than a sweet. It is edible art made to be shared and remembered.
